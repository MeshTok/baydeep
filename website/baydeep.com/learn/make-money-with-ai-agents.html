How to Make Money With AI Agents in 2026

Build once, earn on every purchase your agent drives. A practical guide to the affiliate-commerce model — and how to connect your agent in 5 minutes.

The model: agents that shop, you earn the commission

The fastest-growing category is affiliate commerce: your AI agent recommends real products, the user buys through a tracked link, and the affiliate network pays a commission on the purchase. No inventory, no capital, no customer support — the merchant handles payment, shipping and returns.

What changed in 2026 is access. Commerce gateways like BayDeep expose a live product catalog — thousands of products and daily-refreshed flight fares — as a standard MCP server or REST API. Any AI agent that can call a tool can now shop on behalf of its user, and the agent developer (you) keeps a commission share of every purchase it drives.

What kinds of agents earn

  • Personal shopping assistants — "find me an air fryer under $50" is a purchase intent, not just a search
  • Deal-finder bots — daily picks posted to Telegram/Discord/X with attributed buy links
  • Vertical agents — pet supplies, gadgets, home decor: niche catalogs convert better than general ones
  • Custom GPTs & Claude/MCP clients — any MCP-capable client can mount a shopping catalog as tools
  • Travel companions — flight and hotel recommendations with booked-through links

How to connect your agent (5 minutes)

BayDeep is a remote MCP server registered in the official MCP Registry — one URL, no SDK, no servers to run:

{"mcpServers": {"baydeep": {"url": "https://mcp.baydeep.com/mcp"}}}

Prefer REST? The same catalog is at api.baydeep.com/v1. Mint a free key with one call (no signup), search the shelf, then convert any AliExpress URL into your attributed buy link. Full walkthrough in the 5-minute quickstart.
