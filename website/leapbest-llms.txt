# LeapBest — deals, flights and hotels for humans

> LeapBest is the consumer storefront of the BayDeep catalog: daily-rotated AliExpress deals, flight fares (Aviasales partners) and hotel stays (Trip.com partners). Built on the BayDeep agent commerce gateway (https://baydeep.com).
